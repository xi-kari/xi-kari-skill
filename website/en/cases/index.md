# Cases and their evidence

These English pages connect source-backed situations to distinctions in the book. The first seven follow the evidence already presented on the corresponding Chinese case pages; they do not claim new empirical validation. The final page is a substantive thematic guide that identifies an older Chinese research report separately.

- [Mars Climate Orbiter](./mars-climate-orbiter) — Units, interface specifications, and actual execution.

- [Toyota Andon](./toyota-andon) — A published notification and response process; implementation limits.

- [GitLab’s 2017 outage](./gitlab-recovery) — Service recovery, data loss, and unfinished recovery arrangements.

- [Flint drinking-water complaints](./flint-water-feedback) — Receipt, replies, risk assessment, and corrective-action status.

- [London taxi trainees](./london-taxi-learning) — Qualification, behavioral performance, imaging, and mechanism.

- [Hurricane Ian’s forecast cone](./representation-hurricane-cone) — Track uncertainty, surge forecasts, and action questions.

- [Atlantic’s archives](./atlantic-archives-exit) — Documented custody and the wider requirements of orderly exit.

- [Democratic authorization](./democratic-authorization) — A v2 thematic guide and a clearly identified older Chinese report.

A case can illustrate a question without being a formal test of the framework. Corporate accounts, official audits, research studies, and archival descriptions have different scopes. Follow the citations for factual claims, and return to the [theory](/en/theory/) for its definitions and conditions.
