v3.0 revises the content of v2.1, and the author has approved it in place of v2.1. Its 94 revisions, in sixteen groups, add or tighten the conditions and qualifications of a number of rules and settle several names and notations. No section was added, removed, moved or renamed; the headings of §2.3.1 and §11.9.1 moved from the second to the third level and keep their numbers. v2.1 had reworded v2 without changing its parts, sections or content: its sentences are plainer, and every concept is called by its name; a code stands only at the end of the heading that defines it and in the table in Appendix B. The v2.1 text remains available above for checking citations made against it.

The English reference edition is the author’s translation of v2, revised to the v2.1 wording and then to the v3.0 text. Paragraphs that v3.0 left unchanged keep their translation; the paragraphs v3.0 added or rewrote are new translations, not the author’s own English. The author’s translation of v2 remains available above, unchanged. The previous reference edition, which followed v2.1, is not offered separately; it is included in the Xikari Skill 6.9.0 and 6.10.0 release packages.

The Word file is an earlier v1 artifact, **not a Word edition of v2 or any later text**. Use the Markdown files or the [English reading pages](/en/theory/) for the current text.

The reference edition keeps the translator’s notes. They are identified as translation notes, not additional statements by the author.

## Cite a source section

Include the author, title, theory version, section number, language/edition, and the section link. For example:

> Xikari, *On Conditions, Structure, and Transformation*, theory v3.0 (November 2026), §3.3, English reference edition.

The link to [§3.3](/en/theory/part-03#section-3-3) opens the same numbered section as the [Chinese source](/theory/part-03#section-3-3). For a case’s factual claim, cite the report or study linked from the case page. The site’s guides are derived reading aids and should not be quoted as the author’s original definitions.
