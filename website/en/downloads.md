v2.1 rewords v2 without changing its parts, sections or content: its sentences are plainer, and every concept is called by its name; a code stands only at the end of the heading that defines it and in the table in Appendix B. The English reference edition is the author’s translation of v2, revised in the same way. The author’s translation of v2 remains available above, unchanged.

The Word file is an earlier v1 artifact, **not a v2 Word edition**. Use the Markdown files or the [English reading pages](/en/theory/) for the current text.

The reference edition keeps the translator’s notes. They are identified as translation notes, not additional statements by the author.

## Cite a source section

Include the author, title, theory version, section number, language/edition, and the section link. For example:

> Xikari, *On Conditions, Structure, and Transformation*, theory v2.1 (November 2026), §3.3, English reference edition.

The link to [§3.3](/en/theory/part-03#section-3-3) opens the same numbered section as the [Chinese source](/theory/part-03#section-3-3). For a case’s factual claim, cite the report or study linked from the case page. The site’s guides are derived reading aids and should not be quoted as the author’s original definitions.
