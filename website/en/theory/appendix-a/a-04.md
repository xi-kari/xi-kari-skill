# A.4 HV04 Generative Node (Full Interface Card) {#section-a-4}

A. Identity, Proposition, and Scope

| Field | What to Record |
| --- | --- |
| Interface ID (`id`) | HV04 |
| Qualified ID (`qualified_id`) | `human_variable:HV04` |
| Name (`name`) | Generative Node |
| Claim type (`claim_type`) | H |
| Contract role (`contract_role`) | `human_variable_interface` |
| Proposition (`proposition`) | Generation must be separated into generative condition GC, generative subject GS, and generative event GE. Emergent generation can occur without an identifiable subject. |
| Scope (`scope`) | Formation of new actions, organizations, institutions, or state transitions in human structures |
| Conditions for suspension (`pause_condition`) | Suspend the relevant claims about the subject or the type when a condition is personified, an event is treated as a subject, or an entity is classified as a generative subject without grounds. R0 may still register candidate GC, GS, and GE and a status of unidentified subject separately. Failure to identify a subject does not establish that there is none. |

A=Aggregation level covers units of exposure to conditions, action by subjects, and generative events, with their respective populations of cases, distributions, and aggregation rules. X=Spatial extent covers sites of generation, organizational or platform boundaries, areas of diffusion, and cross-domain environments. E. Uptake, Responsibility, Normative Status, Ceilings, and Correction

| --- | --- |

| Rollback (`rollback`) | Under `appeal_and_rollback_rule`, once rollback is authorized, the designated responsible party must within the stated deadline correct the GC, GS, and GE types and actually remove erroneous attributions of intent or responsibility and their downstream effects. Preserve versions and verification of completion |
