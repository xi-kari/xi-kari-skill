# A.3 HV03 Orientation Anchor (Full Interface Card) {#section-a-3}

A. Identity, Proposition, and Scope

| Field | What to Record |
| --- | --- |
| Interface ID (`id`) | HV03 |
| Qualified ID (`qualified_id`) | `human_variable:HV03` |
| Name (`name`) | Orientation Anchor |
| Claim type (`claim_type`) | H |
| Contract role (`contract_role`) | `human_variable_interface` |
| Proposition (`proposition`) | A goal, identity, memory, commitment, fear, or common problem may first be registered as candidate material about meaning. Register a conditionally effective orientation anchor within the scope of the instance only if an H1-instance provides support on the preselected outcome for resource allocation, action choice, or coordination. |
| Scope (`scope`) | Human structures involving intentionality, coordination, or common problems |
| Conditions for suspension (`pause_condition`) | Do not register a conditionally effective orientation anchor under R1 when there are only slogans, an interpreter’s projections, or coerced uniformity of expression. Sourced expressions, diverse expressions, and risks of coercion may still be registered under R0. Do not infer a common will from them. |

A=Aggregation level covers individual expressions or actions, cases of particular subjects, the population of potential participants, distributions of stances, and aggregation rules. X=Spatial extent covers sites of relationships, organizational spaces, digital public spaces, and the reach of propagation across domains. T=Time span covers the window from expression to action, cycles of commitment, and lags in drift and decoupling. O=Organizational level covers roles in action, teams, organizations, institutions, and public governance ecologies. E. Uptake, Responsibility, Normative Status, Ceilings, and Correction

| Field | What to Record |
| --- | --- |
| Uptake carriers (`carrier`) | 1. Narratives<br>2. Commitments<br>3. Shared memory<br>4. Institutional goals<br>5. Problem definitions |
| Responsible parties (`responsible_subject`) | 1. Those making claims of representativeness. 2. Those allocating resources on that basis |
| Normative status (`normative_status`) | Existence of an anchor does not establish that it is normatively justified |
| Judgment ceiling (`judgment_ceiling`) | Limit R0 and R1 separately. R0 only describes candidate expressions of meaning. When the corresponding H1-instance supports the effect on the selected outcome, R1 may explain a conditional anchor within a limited scope. Do not extrapolate to unselected outcomes |
| Action ceiling (`action_ceiling`) | This variable produces only descriptions of candidate anchors, diverse expressions, comparison results, and needs for more evidence. It does not authorize unifying meaning, representing others’ wishes, or coordinating action. Any real-world adjustment separately requires C12, explicit N premises at runtime, J authorization, and O procedures |
| Counterexamples (`counterexamples`) | 1. A repeated slogan does not change any allocation of resources or action<br>2. Uniform expressions under high pressure conceal genuine goals that conflict |
| Appeal (`appeal`) | Under `appeal_and_rollback_rule`, members can contest representativeness, submit different goals, or refuse to be anchored through a safe, accessible channel with anti-retaliation protection. This triggers review independent of the original chain of judgment about the anchor |
| Rollback (`rollback`) | Under `appeal_and_rollback_rule`, once rollback is authorized, the designated responsible party must within the stated deadline actually revoke the naming of the anchor, remove its force as a representation and its downstream effect on coordination, and restore the state of diverse expression. Preserve versions and verification of completion |
