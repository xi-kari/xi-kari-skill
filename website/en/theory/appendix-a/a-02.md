# A.2 HV02 Boundaries and Interfaces (Full Interface Card) {#section-a-2}

A. Identity, Proposition, and Scope

| Field | What to Record |
| --- | --- |
| Interface ID (`id`) | HV02 |
| Qualified ID (`qualified_id`) | `human_variable:HV02` |
| Name (`name`) | Boundaries and Interfaces |
| Claim type (`claim_type`) | H |

C. Nine-Axis Scale and Object Contract

| Field | What to Record |
| --- | --- |
| Nine-axis scale profile (`scale_profile`) | SP=&lt;A,X,T,O,C,R,I,N,J&gt;. A=Aggregation level covers individual cross-boundary events, cases of interface use, populations by membership category, distributions of admission and refusal, and aggregation rules. X=Spatial extent covers physical points of entry, organizational boundaries, digital interfaces, legal jurisdictions, and cross-domain channels. T=Time span covers the period when a boundary is in force, waiting at interfaces, migration lags, and cycles of reorganization. O=Organizational level covers user roles, gatekeeping teams, organizations, institutions, and governance ecologies. C=Causal level covers cross-boundary events, mechanisms of gatekeeping interaction, interface structure, institutional rules, and systemic conditions. R=Resolution covers original admission and refusal records, sequences of use, individual cases, flow distributions, service metrics, and summaries. Record compression loss as well. I=Scope of influence covers direct users, people excluded, positions where benefits or costs occur indirectly, second-order spillovers, and impacts across domains and generations. N=Network topology scope covers interface nodes, gatekeeping bottlenecks, alternative paths, and cross-domain connections. J=Scope of jurisdiction and authorization requires separate authorization records for inclusion, exclusion, interface changes, and adjustments of rights and responsibilities. Scale elevation requires differences to be recorded axis by axis |
| Effective object (`effective_object`) | A boundary that selectively affects flows of resources, information, rights, or responsibilities |
| Scale invariants (`scale_invariants`) | 1. Positions inside and outside. 2. Cross-boundary channels. 3. Limits of rights and responsibilities |
| Required additions for scale elevation (`required_scale_additions`) | 1. New membership categories<br>2. Interfaces across domains<br>3. Representation and authorization<br>4. Carryover of protections |
| What changes with scale (`changing_semantics`) | 1. Membership, interfaces, and the boundaries of actual control can change |
| Inapplicable objects (`non_applicable_objects`) | 1. Nonhuman boundaries without concepts of membership, rights, or responsibility |
| Prohibited elevations (`forbidden_elevation`) | 1. Treating greater spatial or organizational scope as automatically conferring jurisdiction |

E. Uptake, Responsibility, Normative Status, Ceilings, and Correction

| Field | What to Record |
| --- | --- |
| Uptake carriers (`carrier`) | 1. Membership rules<br>2. Access mechanisms<br>3. Legal or institutional boundaries<br>4. Technical interfaces |
| Responsible parties (`responsible_subject`) | 1. Boundary setters. 2. Interface operators. 3. Parties granting authorization |
| Normative status (`normative_status`) | Facts about a boundary are separate from its normative justification |
| Judgment ceiling (`judgment_ceiling`) | Up to the diagnostic level when evidence about interfaces and impacts is sufficient |
| Action ceiling (`action_ceiling`) | This variable produces only descriptions of boundary states, interface barriers, exclusion risks, and testing needs. It does not authorize changes to admission, exit, rights, or resource flows. Any real-world adjustment separately requires C12, explicit N premises at runtime, J authorization, and O procedures |
| Counterexamples (`counterexamples`) | 1. The formal membership boundary is the opposite of the boundary of actual resource control<br>2. A digital interface is open, but physical, language, or safety barriers prevent some positions from gaining access |
| Appeal (`appeal`) | Under `appeal_and_rollback_rule`, affected people inside and outside the boundary can challenge inclusion, exclusion, and interface barriers through a safe, accessible channel with anti-retaliation protection. This triggers review independent of the original decision chain for the boundary |
| Rollback (`rollback`) | Under `appeal_and_rollback_rule`, once rollback is authorized, the designated responsible party must within the stated deadline correct membership and refusal records, actually restore affected admission rights or interface states, and reverse erroneous boundary actions. Preserve versions and verification of completion |
