# A.8 HV08 Conditional Potential Field (Full Interface Card) {#section-a-8}

A. Identity, Proposition, and Scope

| Field | What to Record |
| --- | --- |
| Interface ID (`id`) | HV08 |
| Qualified ID (`qualified_id`) | `human_variable:HV08` |
| Name (`name`) | Conditional Potential Field |
| Claim type (`claim_type`) | H |
| Contract role (`contract_role`) | `human_variable_interface` |
| Proposition (`proposition`) | Resources, institutions, relationships, power, safety, metrics, platforms, and historical conditions enter a conditional explanation for an outcome family only if they change the single outcome selected in advance for the corresponding route through a detectable mechanism and receive support from the corresponding H4-instance. The outcome is evidence coverage, safety of expression, object behavior, or reflexive response. |
| Scope (`scope`) | Settings in which human action is affected by context, institutions, and positions of power |
| Conditions for suspension (`pause_condition`) | The potential field is treated as an all-purpose background, a subject with intent, or a moral label |

X=Spatial extent covers sites of interaction, organizational or platform boundaries, public digital spaces, and environments across domains. E. Uptake, Responsibility, Normative Status, Ceilings, and Correction

| Field | What to Record |
| --- | --- |
| Uptake carriers (`carrier`) | 1. Institutions<br>2. Resource allocation<br>3. Platforms<br>4. Metrics<br>5. Relationship networks<br>6. Historical accumulations |
| Responsible parties (`responsible_subject`) | 1. Rule makers<br>2. Platform operators<br>3. Resource allocators<br>4. Decision makers for action |
| Normative status (`normative_status`) | Advantages under the conditions or outcomes of systemic selection do not establish normative justification |
| Judgment ceiling (`judgment_ceiling`) | R0 only lists candidate conditions. R1 and R2 form judgments about position-related effects or reflexive responses within the selected H4 outcome and window. | Action ceiling (`action_ceiling`) | This variable produces only descriptions of candidate channels through conditions, differences across positions, masking of evidence, and needs to reduce risk. | Rollback (`rollback`) | Under `appeal_and_rollback_rule`, once rollback is authorized, the designated responsible party must within the stated deadline actually revoke unsupported attribution to a potential field, remove labels on positions and their downstream effects on evaluation and action, and restore the unresolved status. Preserve versions and verification of completion |
