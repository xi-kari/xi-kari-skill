A. Identity, Proposition, and Scope

| Field | What to Record |
| --- | --- |
| Interface ID (`id`) | HV11 |
| Qualified ID (`qualified_id`) | `human_variable:HV11` |
| Name (`name`) | Open-Ended Undertaking |
| Claim type (`claim_type`) | H |
| Contract role (`contract_role`) | `human_variable_interface` |
| Proposition (`proposition`) | Open-ended undertaking examines only actual costs, voluntariness, direction, alternative explanations, and structural consequences. It does not diagnose whether someone has love. |
| Scope (`scope`) | Undertaking in human relationships, organizations, institutions, and public action |

`scale_profile` is the complete nine-axis record SP=&lt;A,X,T,O,C,R,I,N,J&gt;. A single “micro–macro” label cannot stand in for it. A is aggregation level, which must declare units, population, distribution, and aggregation rules. X covers physical space and digital boundaries. T covers windows, lags, and cycles. O is organizational level across roles, teams, organizations, institutions, and governance ecologies. C is causal level across events, interaction mechanisms, meso-level structures, institutions, and systemic conditions. R is resolution across original events, sequences, individual cases, distributions, metrics, and summaries, with compression loss recorded. I is the scope of direct, indirect, second-order, cross-domain, and intergenerational impacts. N is network topology scope. J is scope of jurisdiction and authorization. Expanding A, X, O, or I does not automatically expand J. Observing more cannot confer greater authority to act.

Recorded identities and the object’s identity criterion belong to K in the object contract. Relationships and channels of effect belong in `input_dependencies`, `carrier`, or the causal contract. Membership and affected positions belong in `affected_positions`, `local_exclusion_zone`, and observation fields. None may substitute for a scale axis. The N scale axis is network topology. Below, “explicit N premises at runtime” refers to N1–N5 in the normative choice layer. The two must not be conflated.

All 11 variables share the same limit on action. The variables themselves produce only descriptions, evidence gaps, and requirements for action. They do not authorize real-world adjustments. Their output ceilings are as follows.

The variables must not authorize testing, load reduction, adding resources, repair, protection, attribution of responsibility, exploratory action, advancement, exit arrangements, or other action on their own.
