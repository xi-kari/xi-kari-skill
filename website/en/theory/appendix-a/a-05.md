# A.5 HV05 Action Uptake Layer (Full Interface Card) {#section-a-5}

A. Identity, Proposition, and Scope

| Field | What to Record |
| --- | --- |
| Interface ID (`id`) | HV05 |
| Qualified ID (`qualified_id`) | `human_variable:HV05` |
| Name (`name`) | Action Uptake Layer |
| Claim type (`claim_type`) | H |
| Contract role (`contract_role`) | `human_variable_interface` |
| Proposition (`proposition`) | Uptake carriers CV that execute, transmit, maintain, record, provide care, or repair must be recorded separately from responsible parties RS, cost bearers, beneficiaries, and rights to stop. |
| Scope (`scope`) | Human structures requiring sustained action, maintenance, care, or execution |
| Conditions for suspension (`pause_condition`) | People who execute with limited authority are classified by default as primarily responsible, or the capability to take up work is treated as an obligation |

E. Uptake, Responsibility, Normative Status, Ceilings, and Correction

| Field | What to Record |
| --- | --- |
| Uptake carriers (`carrier`) | 1. Personnel<br>2. Positions<br>3. Procedures<br>4. Facilities<br>5. Institutions |
| Responsible parties (`responsible_subject`) | 1. Parties responsible for conduct<br>2. Parties responsible for decisions<br>3. Parties responsible for authorization<br>4. Parties responsible for oversight<br>5. Parties responsible as beneficiaries<br>6. Parties responsible for remedy |
| Normative status (`normative_status`) | The fact of uptake does not generate a duty to continue taking up the work |
| Judgment ceiling (`judgment_ceiling`) | R0 is limited to classification of uptake and responsibility. R1 is limited to identified functional effects of carriers. R3 is limited to durable traces supported by H5. R4 explains reproduction through carriers under §4.5 and with channel evidence covering subsequent windows. R2 explains historical increments beyond the current representation only with additional G3. Each route is limited by its own materials and window. No route may substitute for the others or create an unlimited duty to take up the work. |
| Action ceiling (`action_ceiling`) | This variable produces only descriptions of CV, RS, costs, capacity, rights to stop, and gaps in uptake, along with needs to reduce load, add resources, or reallocate them. It does not authorize changes in tasks, resource allocation, attribution of responsibility, or protective action. Any real-world adjustment separately requires C12, explicit N premises at runtime, J authorization, and O procedures |
| Counterexamples (`counterexamples`) | 1. The most visible person with limited execution authority is not the party responsible for decisions or authorization, or responsible as a beneficiary<br>2. An automated facility takes up transmission tasks but cannot bear moral or legal responsibility |
| Appeal (`appeal`) | Under `appeal_and_rollback_rule`, people taking up work can challenge tasks, resources, costs, restrictions on the right to stop, and attribution of responsibility through a safe, accessible channel with anti-retaliation protection. This triggers review independent of the original chain of task assignment or attribution |
| Rollback (`rollback`) | Under `appeal_and_rollback_rule`, once rollback is authorized, the designated responsible party must within the stated deadline revoke erroneous task, resource, or responsibility statuses, actually restore previous task and record states, and carry out authorized remedies. Preserve versions and verification of completion |
