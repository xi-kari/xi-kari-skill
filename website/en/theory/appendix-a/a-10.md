# A.10 HV10 Evolutionary Phase (Full Interface Card) {#section-a-10}

A. Identity, Proposition, and Scope

| Field | What to Record |
| --- | --- |
| Interface ID (`id`) | HV10 |
| Qualified ID (`qualified_id`) | `human_variable:HV10` |
| Name (`name`) | Evolutionary Phase |
| Claim type (`claim_type`) | H |
| Contract role (`contract_role`) | `human_variable_interface` |
| Proposition (`proposition`) | S0–S6 and X0 apply only to human intentional collectives that are taking shape or operating. Orientation, generation, uptake, and institutionalization are aspects to be checked. They need not all be mature. Skipping stages, parallelism, mixing, regression, splits, mergers, dormancy, absorption, and functional transfer are possible. |
| Scope (`scope`) | Human intentional collectives that meet the conditions of applicability |
| Conditions for suspension (`pause_condition`) | Suspend the relevant prototype assertion when a stage is given a moral ranking or its title conflicts with the criteria. If an applicability condition is missing, do not draw conclusions about prototypes or paths that depend on it. When all conditions of R0 are met, inapplicability, missing components, and observation needs may still be registered. |
