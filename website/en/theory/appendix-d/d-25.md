# D.25 Education, Knowledge Production, the Professions, and Lifelong Learning {#section-d-25}

In the main text, see §1.8, §6.3, §13, §15.3.3, and §16.16. Research entries cover 25.01–25.08.

Distinguish the aims of education, access to learning, actual knowledge and skills, the interpretation of test results, the award of qualifications, and subsequent opportunities. Specialist instruction and jointly posing questions can coexist. Student participation does not require adopting every proposal. A justified refusal on curricular grounds and the framework’s own `applied` status under GOV-02 belong to different levels. An examination can measure a particular kind of knowledge without thereby establishing that opportunities are fair.

When an assessment is used to determine individual qualifications, checking distributions across groups cannot by itself substitute for evidence about the individual and a review appropriate to the use. Judgments by teachers or experts also need to account for consistency and uncertainty (see OSR, 2021). After correcting a grade record, check whether the correction entered downstream decisions such as admissions in time. Reissuing the grade does not by itself establish that the original opportunity has been restored or that all losses have been made good.

Teaching, family support, and organizational settings work through specific arrangements of time and resources. Sending a notice, activating an account, parents attending a meeting, and improvements in administrative attendance records are distinct from gains in knowledge. Participation can also increase the work of translation, care, and maintenance. The absence of a G3 conditional residual does not rule out learning by ordinary people. Feedback-mediated learning through a carrier and the additional historical increment are each tested under their own contracts.

Trace the stages of knowledge production that matter for the object under study. Examples include setting topics, budgeting, reviewing, practicing, drafting, publishing, and earning returns, as well as making, repairing, editing, translating, and corresponding. There is no requirement that every practice pass through the same gates. Results in submitted papers cannot directly represent research that lacked the resources to produce a draft. Reanalysis of the same data does not constitute independent replication. Professional capabilities, credentials, decision authority, and public trust each require separate evidence. The experience of patients or practitioners can change which questions matter without by itself establishing the overall causal effect.

Teaching example. Trainees acquire new skills, but there are no corresponding jobs locally. This does not establish that the training yielded no learning, and course completion provides no basis for promising employment. Open access for reading, permission to reuse, opportunities to contribute, joint agenda-setting, and recognition of labor are distinct entry points. The absence of records from citizen observers may reflect inadequate coverage. It does not establish that the object is absent locally. Expression and appeal should not depend on prior mastery of the framework’s terminology.

Whether existing skills or qualifications are visible and whether further training is needed are separate questions. Displaying a credential, receiving a response in hiring, securing a job, wages, and net job creation are assessed through separate outcomes. When a correction to a qualification enters admissions criteria in time, it can constitute a real downstream improvement. It does not establish that the final position was secured. Keep the cumulative number established, the number operating in a given year, the number of places set by rule, and the actual number of learners separate. Failure to complete a program does not automatically negate learning in part of it.
