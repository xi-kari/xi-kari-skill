Several other notations recur throughout the book. S*=&lt;B,X,R,I,T,SP,K&gt; denotes the seven elements of the definition of a structural object: in order, boundary, state variables, relations or transition constraints, interface, time window, scale profile, and identity criterion. N0 denotes the null model. K* and F* denote the target identity criterion and the target functional criterion for repair, respectively. G-instance and H-instance refer to a specific test of an empirical question and of a human-domain clause, respectively. The first definition of each in the main text governs its use.

F1–F6 are the identifiers of the conditional formal propositions. Used on their own, F and F* follow their local definitions and can denote a dynamic update, a functional criterion, or a repair target; use of the same letter does not establish that the object is the same. The historical increment (G3) tests only a historical conditional increment beyond the given current representation. Learning, accumulation, sustained effects, and intertemporal reproduction mediated by carriers are tested under the conditions of their respective mechanisms; positive support from a historical-increment test is not a common prerequisite for them.
