# Appendix B Numbering Scheme and Glossary {#part-title-18}

Several other symbols recur throughout the book. `S*=<B,X,R,I,T,SP,K>` gives the seven fields of a D0 candidate object contract (boundary, state variables, relations or transition constraints, interface, time window, scale profile, and identity criterion). `N0` is the null model. `K*/F*` are the target identity criterion and the target functional criterion for repair, respectively. `G-instance` and `H-instance` denote specific test instances for empirical question families and human-domain clauses, respectively. The first definition of each in the main text governs its use.

F1–F6 are identifiers for formal propositions. Used on their own, F and F* can denote a dynamic update, a functional criterion, or a repair target according to their local definitions. Use of the same letter does not establish that the object is the same. G3 tests only historical conditional increments beyond the given current representation. When mediated by a carrier, learning, accumulation, sustained effects, and intertemporal reproduction are tested under their respective mechanism contracts. They do not all require positive support from G3.
