# Concepts through examples

These pages explain distinctions in the theory through a small number of worked examples. They are reading aids, with links back to the complete source. Constructed models and documented cases retain their different evidential roles.

- [Receiving a message is not yet effective feedback](./feedback) — Trace the return and its subsequent effect.

- [Records, learning, and additional historical information](./history) — Separate formation, current carriers, and historical increments.

- [Two effective links can still fail as a whole](./chain) — Check joint response instead of multiplying averages.

- [An accurate average can miss local overload](./representation) — Ask which distinctions the task needs.

- [Each task is feasible; the combined plan may not be](./joint-feasibility) — Check shared resources, timing, and common actions.

- [Doing the work does not settle all responsibility](./responsibility) — Separate uptake carriers from responsible parties.

- [Recovery with support is still recovery within its scope](./recovery) — Name the function, people, support, and period.

- [Predicting ordinary behavior is not enough for intervention](./prediction) — Test new operations and outcome targets separately.
