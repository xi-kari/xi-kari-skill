# How to use this reading site

The main reading path is the complete English reference edition of **Xikari: On Conditions, Structure, and Transformation**, theory v2, November 2026. Its 397 numbered sections preserve the order of the Chinese original, including all sixteen parts, Appendices A–D, and the references. The [Chinese source](/theory/) remains authoritative when wording differs.

## Begin with one complete section

Open the [book contents](/en/) or [scope and reading instructions](/en/theory/). Read the whole section before its guide. The author’s text and the site’s explanatory material have separate presentation. “Source only” hides the guides without changing the source.

Useful starting points are [§3.3 Effective feedback](/en/theory/part-03#section-3-3), [§4.5 History and current carriers](/en/theory/part-04#section-4-5), and [C.7 Representational sufficiency and actual execution](/en/theory/appendix-c#section-c-7). A formal example can establish what follows from its stated assumptions; it does not establish that a real organization satisfies those assumptions.

## What has been translated

The complete theory and **all 421 on-page Chinese reading lessons** are available in English. The lessons retain the explanations, argument steps, distinctions, teaching examples, documented cases, counterexamples, questions and answers, and source notes of their Chinese counterparts. The site also includes fourteen [concept pages](/en/concepts/), eight [case pages](/en/cases/), and a [lesson directory](./selected-guides).

Use the language switch to move between corresponding source sections and lessons. Original figures have English captions and descriptions. Downloadable historical research manuscripts, source registers, excerpts, and diagram files remain in Chinese and are labeled accordingly. Statements about sources read and their dates preserve the original Chinese lessons’ research records. The democratic-authorization case page identifies its older Chinese research report separately.

## Follow a question through the book

Search can locate a term, a case, or a phrase. Read the surrounding passage after opening a result: the same word can have different ordinary and technical uses. The [numbering and terminology index](/en/theory/appendix-b) helps distinguish D definitions, G empirical question families, F formal propositions, human-variable interfaces, and governance provisions.

For a practical reading sequence, start with the question you need answered. [Feedback](../concepts/feedback) concerns a return channel’s effect; [history](../concepts/history) concerns formation, retention, later use, and any additional historical increment; [responsibility](../concepts/responsibility) concerns separately justified duties and authority. Completing one inquiry does not finish the others.

## Use the cases as evidence with limits

Each case separates what its cited sources record from the site’s interpretation. A company’s account of its own process is useful evidence of that account, but cannot by itself estimate implementation rates across every facility. An accident report can identify a documented failure without becoming a pre-registered test of this framework. The cases do not collectively validate the theory.

Constructed scenarios are labeled as teaching examples. Their numbers are assumptions or calculations, not observations of real people. No reading-site example grants permission to intervene in someone’s life, workplace, or institution.

## Continue with Xikari Skill

When using an installed skill, name it explicitly and describe the question, available materials, desired output, and practical constraints. For example:

> Use xi-kari-skill to examine our complaint process. We receive reports, but the same faults recur. Here are the intake records, handling rules, and recent changes. Identify what the evidence supports, compare explanations, and recommend a concrete improvement with its limits.

This request leaves the cause open. The skill’s full theory-learning and evidence responsibilities still apply; a short website guide does not replace them. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/xi-kari/xi-kari-skill/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md) and [releases](https://github.com/xi-kari/xi-kari-skill/releases/latest).

## Save and cite

Use [Downloads and citation](/en/downloads) for the English reading edition, the original supplied English file, and the Chinese authority. Cite the author, title, theory version, and section number. For factual claims about a case, cite the underlying report or study rather than treating the theory as its evidence.
