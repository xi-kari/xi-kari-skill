# Project introduction surface

Mode: Persuade, with an Operate research directory. The page stands alone and inherits nothing from the blog or the earlier introduction.

Audience and job: a Chinese-reading visitor arriving from the repository decides whether to install the Skill or read the book. Proof on hand: the eight routes and eleven modules in `SKILL.md`, the stated boundaries in `README.md`, the release archive, the generated-output screenshots and the published research status.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Skill is drawn as an instrument approach chart. A question enters, flies a published route through numbered fixes, reaches a decision point, and either lands as a written judgment or goes around. The page refuses the feature-card product page and the quiet scholarly sheet.

OWN-WORLD: White chart paper, blue-black procedure ink, cobalt airspace tint, a magenta line reserved for the active route, taxiway-sign yellow for direction, red plates for refusals. Heavy sans speaks for the Skill, Song for quoted source text, monospace for identifiers. Boxed data, square corners; line style (solid, dashed, dotted) carries epistemic state.

STORY: The visitor picks their own question in plain words, watches its route flown from entry to RWY 09 成文, reads what kind of judgment that route may deliver and what it will not claim, then downloads the release or opens the book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A briefing strip of identity facts, then a full-width plan view: the source text as a compass-rose navaid beside the runway, one arc carrying M3–M7 and M10, the trunk M0–M2 inbound on 090°, question entries at the chart edge. Headline top left at display scale with the download sign beside it; a legs readout names the selected route's fixes and the judgment it delivers.

FORM: Aeronautical approach plate, candidate 7 of 7 on the grounded list; seed d4747691. Code-led. Raises taken from the dealt hand: staged emergence with an irreversible registration mark (darkroom); state by line style (exposure record); entry by the visitor's own question (choice frame); flat fields at page scale (record sleeve); a route that re-plans when a condition flips (rain garden); one arc ruling the opening (curved crease). Signature interaction: choose a question and the route is flown; flip its condition and the route re-plans.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
