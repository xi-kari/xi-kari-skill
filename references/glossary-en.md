# Chinese–English Glossary

English renderings used by the reference edition in `source/en/`, listed in order of first appearance in the Chinese source. *Locked* renderings are settled; *proposed* ones are used consistently but await the author's decision. A term keeps its rendering only in its specialized sense; ordinary uses take ordinary English. Where a rendering seems to conflict with the Chinese source, the Chinese governs.
