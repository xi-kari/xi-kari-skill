# Xikari

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Readers who want to understand the concepts in Xikari's *条件结构与转化论*, read the complete work, look up a specific distinction, or use the accompanying Skill.

## Product Purpose

Connect concepts to concrete questions, explanatory diagrams and sourced real examples, with a clear route back to the complete original text. Support both continuous reading and concept lookup.

## Operating Context

The repository contains the authoritative Markdown, host-facing Skill resources and an existing public project introduction. The handbook is a separate reading surface within that project website.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The original text retains its identity and content. Teaching explanations, historical research and new case analyses have distinct source identities. Research materials can support case development; they do not become current-theory evaluations merely by being included. Work products and research handoffs remain outside the installed Skill.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Xikari name and the established blue-white project identity. The reading experience prioritizes clear Chinese typography, diagrams, accessible navigation and complete content.

## Evidence on Hand

The complete theory is in `source/`. Existing Skill guides describe supported use. Public historical case discussions and local research packages provide leads for case development. Claims about real events require identifiable sources.

## Product Principles

- Let a concrete question introduce a concept.
- Explain the distinction a concept makes, and the limits of that distinction.
- Keep the original text accessible from every teaching route.
- Make diagrams carry an explanation rather than decoration.
- Keep published prose free of production notes and iteration logs.
